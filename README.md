# india crypto launch playbook 🇮🇳

how i'd launch a crypto product in india from zero, on a small budget.

written from years of doing community, KOLs, events and BD for crypto, fintech and AI brands in india. no theory, just what tends to work here.

> india isn't one market. treat it like ten.

## the playbook

| # | chapter | the one-line version |
|---|---|---|
| 01 | [pick a city, not a country](playbook/01-city-first.md) | win one city, then copy-paste |
| 02 | [go where people already are](playbook/02-existing-communities.md) | telegram & whatsapp groups before your own discord |
| 03 | [campus is your cheapest channel](playbook/03-campus.md) | one good campus ambassador > 10 paid posts |
| 04 | [KOLs for trust, not reach](playbook/04-kols.md) | small creators who explain > big accounts who post a link |
| 05 | [IRL every month](playbook/05-irl.md) | a 40 person meetup beats a 4,000 person space |
| 06 | [make the first transaction stupid simple](playbook/06-first-transaction.md) | if they have to google it, you lost them |
| 07 | [hackathons for retention](playbook/07-hackathons.md) | the work starts after demo day |
| 08 | [3 numbers, ignore the rest](playbook/08-metrics.md) | weekly actives, week-2 return, referral share |
| 09 | [talk about compliance openly](playbook/09-trust-and-compliance.md) | one honest tax post > any campaign |
| 10 | [be in the comments](playbook/10-be-in-the-comments.md) | founders replying in public is free growth |

## who this is for
founders, marketers and community leads launching a crypto / web3 product for indian users with more hustle than budget.

## contribute
launched something in india and learned the hard way? open a PR with your lesson. real examples > opinions.
