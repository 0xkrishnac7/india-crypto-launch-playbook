# 01 · pick a city, not a country

bangalore devs, mumbai traders and tier 2 college students want completely different things. one message won't land with all three.

**do this**
- pick one city where your first users already are (look at your waitlist, socials, early signups)
- pick one persona in that city: builders, traders, students or creators
- go deep: 3 meetups, 2 campus sessions, 1 local partner, all in the same city within 60 days
- only expand when people in city #1 start bringing friends without being asked

**watch out for**
- "pan-india launch" with a pan-india budget of ₹2 lakh. that's just noise spread thin
- copying a delhi playbook into chennai. language, culture and timing change everything

**signal it's working:** people from city #1 ask "when are you coming to my city?"
