# 10 · be in the comments

founders and team members replying to users in public is the most underrated growth channel in crypto.

**do this**
- spend 20 minutes a day replying to mentions, questions and complaints
- reply to complaints fastest. a fixed problem in public is better marketing than a launch video
- reply under bigger accounts talking about your space, with something useful (not a link)
- thank people who make content about you, by name

**watch out for**
- defensive replies. acknowledge, fix, follow up
- outsourcing your voice entirely. people can tell
