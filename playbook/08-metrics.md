# 08 · track 3 numbers, ignore the rest

| metric | why |
|---|---|
| weekly active users | are real people using it, not just signing up? |
| week-2 return rate | % of new users who come back in their second week. the best early signal of product-market fit |
| referral share | % of new users who came from another user. tells you if people actually like it |

everything else (followers, impressions, discord members, "community size") is vanity until these move.

**weekly ritual:** one slide, these 3 numbers, what changed, and one experiment for next week.
