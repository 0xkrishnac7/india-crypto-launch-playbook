# 04 · KOLs for trust, not reach

indian users trust people who explain things in their language, not accounts who drop a link with 🚀.

**do this**
- prioritise creators who teach (explainers, tutorials, walkthroughs), especially in hindi and regional languages
- pay for a series (3-4 pieces over a month), not one post. trust compounds
- give them early access and let them be honest, including the downsides
- track signups and week-2 activity per creator, not views

**watch out for**
- follower count as the only metric. check comments: are real people asking real questions?
- undisclosed paid promos. disclose. your brand's trust is on the line too

**brief template (keep it short)**
- what the product does in one sentence
- the one action you want viewers to take
- 3 things they must mention, 3 things they must never say
- deadline, deliverables, payment terms
