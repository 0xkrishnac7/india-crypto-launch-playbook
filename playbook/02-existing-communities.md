# 02 · go where people already are

a brand new discord with 40 members feels like an empty restaurant. people join busy places.

**do this**
- list 20 existing telegram / whatsapp / discord groups your users already hang out in
- show up as a helpful member first. answer questions, share useful stuff, no links for 2 weeks
- offer value to admins: an AMA, a workshop, a bounty for their members
- build your own community only once you have ~100 people who'd actually show up

**watch out for**
- spamming groups with your link. you'll get banned and remembered for the wrong reason
- measuring "members" instead of "people who talk"
