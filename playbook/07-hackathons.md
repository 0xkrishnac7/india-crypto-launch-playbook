# 07 · hackathons are for retention, not just leads

most hackathon projects die within a week of demo day. that's where your opportunity is.

**do this**
- judge and mentor, don't just sponsor a banner
- set clear, stable rules on day 1 (changing T&Cs mid-event kills trust fast)
- offer post-event support: small grants, office hours, intros to investors and users
- check in with promising teams at day 7, 30 and 90

**watch out for**
- asking winners for equity or a cut of their round. give prizes and intros, not a term sheet
- measuring success by "number of submissions"

**the metric that matters:** how many teams are still building on you 6 months later
