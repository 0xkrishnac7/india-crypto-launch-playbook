# 03 · campus is your cheapest growth channel

blockchain and tech clubs at IITs, NITs and tier 2 colleges are full of curious people with time.

**do this**
- reach out to club leads, not college admins
- bring what they actually need: mentors, swag, a real bounty with real prize money, and a certificate they can put on linkedin
- run a hands-on workshop (people should leave having made their first transaction / deploy)
- pick 1-2 campus ambassadors per college who are already active, and give them a clear monthly goal

**watch out for**
- "ambassador programs" that are just referral spam
- one-off events with no follow up. the follow up is where retention happens

**rule of thumb:** one great campus ambassador > 10 paid posts
