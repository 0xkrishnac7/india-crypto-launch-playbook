# 09 · talk about compliance openly

indian users worry about KYC, TDS, taxes and "will this get banned". silence makes it worse.

**do this**
- write one honest, plain-language pinned post: how KYC works on your product, what taxes apply, what records users should keep
- update it when rules change, and say what changed
- link to official sources where you can
- have your support team answer tax/KYC questions the same way every time

**why it works:** one honest pinned post on taxes builds more trust than any campaign.

*not legal or tax advice. get proper advice for your product and keep the post accurate.*
