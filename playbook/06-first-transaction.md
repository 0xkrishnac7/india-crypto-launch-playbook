# 06 · make the first transaction stupid simple

if a user has to google anything during onboarding, you've lost them.

**do this**
- INR on-ramp that feels familiar (UPI-like flows where possible)
- explain fees upfront, in rupees
- tell users what happens with taxes and KYC before they ask
- walk 5 real users through onboarding while you watch silently. fix every place they pause

**watch out for**
- crypto jargon in the first 3 screens (gas, slippage, seed phrase) without a plain-language explanation
- "connect wallet" as step one for people who've never had a wallet
