# 05 · do something IRL every month

a 40 person meetup with good food beats a 4,000 person twitter space.

**do this**
- one small, consistent event per month in your focus city: same week, same format, good food
- keep it practical: a demo, a workshop, a founder AMA, a "bring your laptop" build session
- collect contacts properly (a simple form or QR), and follow up within 48 hours
- partner with existing communities and co-working spaces to share costs and audiences

**why it works:** the people who show up become your first mods, testers, ambassadors and evangelists.

**watch out for:** big expensive one-offs with no follow up. consistency > size.
